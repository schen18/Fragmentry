# 🌌 Fragmentry: A Poet’s Manual

Welcome to **Fragmentry**, a quiet, atmospheric space designed for the non-linear capture and synthesis of thought fragments. This manual will guide you through the void, from the first spark of an idea to the weaving of a finished poem.

---

## 🏛️ The Landscape (Screens)

### 1. The Field (Browsing)
The central anchor where all your fragments settle.
*   **The Nebula Clusters**: Fragments are automatically grouped by their shared **Motifs** (themes). For focus, all clusters are **collapsed by default**.
*   **Persistent Toggle**: Tap any Nebula Tag (#motif) to expand or collapse its cluster. Your choices are remembered even after you close the app.
*   **The Semantic Star**: Above each fragment and tag floats a small particle—a "Semantic Star." Its horizontal position represents the thought's "semantic tone." Thoughts with similar meanings will align their stars into vertical corridors.
*   **Stable Reading**: While the stars drift meaningfully, the fragment text remains centered and stable for maximum readability.
*   **Seek (Search)**: Tap "the field" at the top to filter the void. Clusters automatically expand during search to reveal matches.

### 2. The Spark (Continuous Capture)
A dedicated "Void Mode" for pure thought capture.
*   **Zero Friction**: Focus entirely on your text. Navigation is hidden to maintain focus.
*   **Continuous Flow**: Tapping "capture" preserves your thought and clears the void instantly for your next spark, allowing you to capture entire streams of consciousness without leaving the screen.
*   **Return**: Use the system back button or "cancel" to return to the field.

### 3. The Echo (Exploration & Focus)
Accessed by tapping any fragment. The focused thought takes center stage.
*   **The Soloist**: Your focused fragment. If collected, it emanates a soft amber glow and text shadow.
*   **The Chorus (Resonances)**: Below, resonant peer thoughts drift into view with a slow, spectral horizontal movement. These are semantically related thoughts discovered by on-device AI.
*   **Motif Highlighting**: Recurrent concepts appear in **AmberPatina**. Tapping a motif instantly opens its dedicated **Thread**.
*   **Interactive Status**: The "collect" label at the top glows amber when a fragment is part of your assembly, providing an instant visual confirmation.

### 4. The Thread (Constellation View)
A focused, vertical stream of every fragment sharing a specific motif. It is the purest way to explore a single theme in depth.

### 5. The Assembly (The Collector)
Your curated workspace for weaving shards into poems.
*   **The Motif Cloud**: The top features a "tag cloud" of motifs within your collection. Sizes scale dynamically based on frequency. **Click any motif** in the cloud to view its full Thread.
*   **Linear Field**: Shards are presented in a readable vertical sequence while maintaining their horizontal **Semantic Gravity**, creating a structured yet organic flow.
*   **The Weave**: Tap "weave" to copy all collected text to your clipboard, separated by stanzas.
*   **The Preserve**: Tap "preserve" to save your assembly as a `.txt` file.

### 6. The Veil (System)
A quiet space for maintenance and migration.
*   **Preserve/Summon**: Export or restore your entire library as a JSON backup.
*   **Migrate**: Import fragments from an external text file. 
    *   **Format**: Separate fragments with `___` on a new line. 
    *   **Intelligence**: The app preserves your internal poem structure (line breaks/stanzas) while removing outer whitespace.
    *   **Progress**: A modal will show the real-time progress of creating shards and generating their unique semantic embeddings.

## ✨ Primary Interactions

| Action | Interaction | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spark** | Type + "Capture" | Preserve a new thought in the void. |
| **Collapse/Expand** | Tap Tag (#motif) | Toggle visibility of a thematic cluster in the Field. |
| **Dissolve** | Long-press (Field/Thread) | Thematically return a fragment to the void (Delete). |
| **Uncollect** | Long-press (Assembly) | Remove a fragment from your curated collection without deleting it. |
| **Explore** | Tap Motif Cloud | Navigate from Assembly to a specific thematic Thread. |
| **Collect** | Tap "Collect" (Echo) | Pin a fragment to your Assembly; its star and text will begin to glow. |

## ✍️ The Poet’s Flow: From Spark to Poem

### Phase I: The Gathering (Capture)
Enter **The Spark**. Don't worry about structure. Type a line, tap capture, type the next. Let the fragments accumulate in the void.

### Phase II: The Wandering (Refinement)
Return to **The Field**. Clusters are collapsed, keeping the void calm. Expand a Nebula that draws you in. Notice how the **Semantic Stars** align into vertical corridors, helping you find related tones spatially.

### Phase III: The Resonance (Exploration)
Tap a fragment to enter **The Echo**. Watch the **Chorus** drift—the AI surfaces forgotten connections. Tap a highlighted **Motif** to follow a specific thematic **Thread**.

### Phase IV: The Curation (Collection)
Tap **Collect** on fragments that vibrate together. They will gain a **Luminous Shimmer** (amber glow), signaling they are ready for the assembly.

### Phase V: The Synthesis (The Weave)
Navigate to **The Assembly**. The **Motif Cloud** at the top summarizes your collection's essence. Long-press any shards that no longer fit to uncollect them. Once the assembly feels complete, tap **Weave** to bring your poem out of the void.
